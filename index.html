Glossary

Definitions

Demand and routing
Current uses R0; A1 and A2 use R0(1+g). a is the A2 store-received share, independent of N. r is the final local-restock share of store receipts.
Capacity
h = h0 + r hL + (1−r) hP. A is staff minutes after existing duties and exception reserve. Group utilisation = k a R1 h / (N A); the default k = 2 is a concentration assumption, not a measured store maximum.
Route costs
cD covers variable direct warehouse processing. cT includes company-paid store-to-warehouse transport and variable warehouse processing. Both exclude store labour and M. cL is local non-labour cost.
Zero-work cases
No demand, no store receipts, zero modelled work or zero available capacity does not determine a primary minimum. The auxiliary count can still show fixed policy costs.
Scope
Warehouse capacity is assumed sufficient. A minimum above 851 is unavailable within the network, not clipped to 851. Customer convenience and refund waiting are not predicted by this cost model.
